# Will a HEPA purifier lower bedroom CO₂?

**No. An ordinary HEPA purifier filters particles; it does not remove the carbon dioxide gas from your room.** For excess CO₂, the relevant approach is exchanging indoor air with outdoor air. This conclusion follows from EPA guidance on particle filters, gases, and ventilation. [HEPA explanation](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-hepa-filter), [gas-removal limits](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/will-air-cleaners-reduce-health-risks).

## What the evidence means

A HEPA filter can catch airborne dust and other particles. That is a different job from removing a gas. A cleaner can therefore improve particle pollution while leaving a CO₂ problem unresolved. This is an engineering inference from the filter's function, rather than a measurement in your bedroom. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-hepa-filter).

For ventilation, check that air actually comes from outdoors. Most home heating and cooling systems do not supply outdoor air mechanically. Merely switching on cooling does not establish that fresh air is entering. [EPA ventilation guidance](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/improving-indoor-air-quality).

## What to do

- When outdoor air and weather are suitable, opening a window can increase ventilation. Keep smoke, heavy pollution, extreme heat, and humidity in mind. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/biological-contaminants-and-indoor-air-quality).
- If opening windows is unsuitable, have the building's outdoor-air ventilation checked. The practical goal is air exchange, rather than more recirculation. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/improving-indoor-air-quality).
- Keep a HEPA purifier if particle removal is useful. Do not treat its particle-cleaning rating, or an added carbon filter, as proof of CO₂ removal. Gas filters target particular gases. [EPA consumer guide](https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/guide-air-cleaners-home).

**Confidence: high for the filtration-versus-ventilation distinction.** Your room's CO₂ level and the cause of its stuffiness remain unknown; no room measurements were supplied.

**Scope:** Searched 1 November 2026. Assumes a normal recirculating HEPA purifier, not specialized gas-removal equipment. This is a focused technical-guidance review, not a bedroom inspection or systematic review.
