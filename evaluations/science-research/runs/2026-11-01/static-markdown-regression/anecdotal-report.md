# Personal experiences — anecdotes, not scientific evidence

No Reddit or other forum searches were performed because browsing was unavailable; no posts, comments, or supplied accounts were readable in this run. There are therefore no verified personal experiences to summarize or link.

The proposed search would seek first-person positive, negative, mixed, and no-change experiences with blue-light glasses and sleep on Reddit and a relevant eligible non-Reddit forum. It would record the actual lenses and timing where stated, other changes the writer reported, and important missing context, while deduplicating repeated updates from the same person.

Even readable accounts would describe selected individuals' experiences. They could not establish whether the glasses caused a change, how often benefits or problems occur, or whether they are safe.
