## Limits

- Model instructions guide source selection and appraisal; they are not a technical browsing restriction.
- Search limits, publisher access failures, and CAPTCHAs affected the live examples. Reports expose those gaps instead of filling them from snippets.
- Native Claude and ChatGPT installation and execution remain untested.
- Saved examples are development snapshots, not individualized advice or maintained current reviews. Synthetic links must never be treated as real citations.
- [behavioral-cases.md](behavioral-cases.md) includes further regression scenarios; they are specifications, not claims of completed runs.
