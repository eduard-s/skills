# Separate personal-experience report

Show practical experiences and questions worth checking, not what usually happens or a scientific conclusion.

## Collection

- Use only hosts and paths under Anecdotal candidates in `source-policy.md`, or named forum additions explicitly approved by the user for this run.
- Search neutral terms plus positive, negative, mixed, and no-change terms. Look on Reddit and an eligible non-Reddit forum when relevant. Readable firsthand content matters more than filling a quota.
- Open the original post and the specific comment where possible. Record permalink, community, publication date if visible, access date, and how much of the thread could be read.
- Prefer relevant first-person descriptions. Label second-hand accounts and generally omit them. Exclude clear advertising, affiliate promotions, copied stories, bots, and inaccessible relevant content.
- Never invent age, dose, diagnosis, timing, test result, outcome, or expertise. Say “not stated” when a missing detail matters. Avoid unnecessary usernames and identifying details.
- Deduplicate cross-posts and repeated updates from one person. Do not claim the results are representative or balanced if one kind of account was missing.
- If access is blocked, state what could not be read. Snippets and an inaccessible URL do not become a verified case. An honest “no accessible cases” report is valid.

## Interpretation

- Paraphrase briefly and link each case/thread directly. Prefer no quotations; comply with the host tool's quotation limits.
- Report what the writer experienced and attributed it to. Do not endorse their causal explanation, diagnosis, dose, workaround, or medical advice.
- Upvotes, replies, and the fraction of posts saying something are not effectiveness or adverse-event rates. Even an accurate count describes only the selected posts.
- Briefly explain self-selection, uncertain identities/details, expectations, natural changes, other exposures, missing comparisons, and possible undisclosed promotion.
- Accounts that conflict with science stay in this report. They can suggest research questions; they cannot change scientific confidence or justify unsupported recommendations.

## Output

Create `anecdotal-report.md`, or a separate chat section if files are unavailable. Title it **Personal experiences — anecdotes, not scientific evidence**. Include:

1. One sentence on searches and readable material.
2. A compact table or up to five short bullets: reported experience, useful context, direct link, and relevant missing information. Avoid a table for a single case.
3. A brief limitations paragraph and at most two questions worth checking against research.

Aim for 150–300 words when cases exist. Use fewer words for no cases or blocked access; never pad. Link back to the scientific brief without combining evidence bases.
